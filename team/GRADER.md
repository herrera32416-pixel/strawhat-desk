# GRADER SOP
**Purpose:** a ledger of every pick and lean (plus historical teasers, retired 2026-10-08), settled daily from ESPN finals and box scores.
- **Logging:** Board picks are logged the first time they show, at the shown price. Props are logged once per game when kick is within 24h (so London 8:30am games are caught by the Saturday run); the game's top 5 are locked at the latest pulled prices. Teasers: the Saturday CFB set (5 tickets) is logged on the Saturday 9am run and the Sunday NFL set on the Sunday 9am run, once each, keyed by set + date, before the first leg kicks.
- **Settling:** an item settles as soon as ESPN marks its game final (no fixed delay). The ESPN scoreboard is matched by date and team names (orientation-safe).
  - Spread/total/ML are graded on final scores.
  - Props are graded from the ESPN summary box score. A player who appears anywhere in the box score (any category, incl. defense/returns) but has no line in the prop's category is graded as **0** (e.g. rec yds 0, anytime TD no). Only a player absent from the box score entirely (DNP) is VOID (0u).
  - Historical teasers/parlays (retired 2026-10-08; no new ones are logged) are graded leg by leg as each game finishes: the ticket is **LOST as soon as any leg loses**, even if other legs haven't played. Pushes reduce it per the DK table (`DK6`, stored as `price` per ticket size).
- **Official vs research:** every item has `kind`. Official = board picks (+ historical PLAY teaser tickets); research = props (+ historical PASS teaser tickets). Headlines `official` and `research` are shown separately on the site; props also split anytime TD vs yards/receptions with expected wins (model % vs market % summed over settled W/L).
- **Units:** 1u flat ($20). A win pays at the logged American price. Headline record and units are shown per tab. The teaser headlines still exist in the data for history but are no longer shown on the site.

- **VOID (pulled before kickoff):** an item keeps its original stamp, price and fields. It gets `status=VOID`, `units=0`, `void_reason` and `voided_ct`. This happens when (a) a pre-kick recheck drops a board pick, a teaser leg/ticket, or a prop on a player newly Out, or (b) a rule change removes an open pick before kickoff (2026-10-03: open ML picks). VOIDs are excluded from W-L-P and units and shown as a separate count.
