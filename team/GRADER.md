# GRADER SOP
**Purpose:** a ledger of every pick, lean and teaser, settled daily from ESPN finals and box scores.
- **Logging:** Board picks are logged the first time they show, at the shown price. Props are logged once per game when kick is within 14h; the game's top 5 are locked. Teasers: a set of 5 is logged once per slate day when the first leg is within 14h.
- **Settling:** an item becomes eligible 4h after kick. The ESPN scoreboard is matched by date and team names (orientation-safe).
  - Spread/total/ML are graded on final scores.
  - Props are graded from the ESPN summary box score. A player absent from the box score is VOID (0u).
  - Teasers are graded leg by leg: any loss loses the ticket, and pushes reduce it per the DK table.
- **Units:** 1u flat ($20). A win pays at the logged American price. Headline record and units are shown per tab, plus the teaser ticket-#1 rule separately.
