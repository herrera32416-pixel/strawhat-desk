Strawhat Desk NFL + college football · picks only · 1u = $20 · DK / Betr / Bovada

What this desk is

A market-anchored desk, not a power-ratings desk. Earlier work on DESK (desk-v1) showed that blending FPI/public ratings into the closing line gets a fitted weight of about 0, so this desk does not try to out-rate the market. It does three things instead:

  1. Shop: take the consensus fair price from every other book (Pinnacle plus about 20 US/EU books, read off the Odds API us,eu regions) and compare it to DraftKings and Bovada.
  2. Price lines through key numbers: the KEYS role turns any line into win/push/loss odds using empirical NFL/CFB margin and total distributions (nflverse closes 1999–2025, ESPN closes 2022–25). That handles alt lines, teased lines and the gap between DK's line and the consensus line.
  3. Teasers: score each 6-point leg's cover probability at its teased number, then build tickets.

Betr is not on The Odds API, so no Betr price is shown. Model % is always our fair probability at the exact DK/Bovada line, and Mkt % is that book's own two-way no-vig. PICK means EV ≥ 2% at a real price. Every number comes from a source, and blanks stay blank.

Rules

  • Board: PICK when EV ≥ 2% vs fair, at least 3 reference books, fair within 6pp of the book's own no-vig (guards against stale lines), and for ML a price between −300 and +300. Picks are logged the first time they show.
  • Props: top 5 per game by model − market. Logged once per game when kick is within 24h, as paper leans. Game-day runs refresh lines older than 12h when the budget allows.
  • Teasers: 5 tickets × 6 legs × 6 pts. Each leg is used at most 2× across tickets, and a ticket never has two legs from one game. Ticket #1 is the backtested rule. A set is logged once per slate day (first leg within 14h).
  • Grading: ESPN finals and box scores. A player missing from the box score is VOID.

Credits

The Odds API budget is a hard cap of 40 credits/day: odds cost 6 per sport (us+eu × ML/spread/total), and props cost 1 per market per game, round-robined across games within 54h of kick. The build used 1,240 credits for historical snapshots.
