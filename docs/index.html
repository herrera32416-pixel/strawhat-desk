Strawhat Desk NFL + college football · picks only · 1u = $20 · DK / Betr / Bovada

What this desk is

A market-anchored desk, not a power-ratings desk. Earlier work on DESK (desk-v1) showed that blending FPI/public ratings into the closing line gets a fitted weight of about 0, so this desk does not try to out-rate the market. It does two things instead:

  1. Shop: take the consensus fair price from every other book (Pinnacle plus about 20 US/EU books, read off the Odds API us,eu regions) and compare it to DraftKings and Bovada.
  2. Price lines through key numbers: the KEYS role turns any line into win/push/loss odds using empirical NFL/CFB margin and total distributions (nflverse closes 1999–2025, ESPN closes 2022–25). That handles alt lines and the gap between DK's line and the consensus line.

Betr is not on The Odds API, so no Betr price is shown. Model % is always our fair probability at the exact DK/Bovada line, and Mkt % is that book's own two-way no-vig. PICK means EV ≥ 3% at a real price (raised from 2% on Oct 3, 2026). ML is reference-only. Every number comes from a source, and blanks stay blank.

Rules

Official plays = board PICKs. Research = props. They are scored separately on every tab and in the ledger.

  • Board: PICK when EV ≥ 3% vs fair, at least 3 reference books, and fair within 6pp of the book's own no-vig (guards against stale lines). No PICK when the fair line and the book's line are more than 3 points apart (spread center or total). No spread or total PICK when the game's |spread| ≥ 30. ML is reference-only: model % and EV are shown, but ML is never a pick. Picks are logged the first time they show.
  • Pre-kick recheck (driven by kickoff times): runs at :15/:45 but only does anything when an official pick or logged prop kicks within ~70 minutes and that window hasn't been rechecked yet. On a normal Sunday that is about 11:15am CT (noon kicks), ~2:15pm (3:05/3:25) and ~6:15pm (7:20). It re-pulls only those games (4 credits per window, at most 3 windows/day, reserved out of the props budget). Props cost nothing to recheck: ESPN's injury report is read and a player now Out/IR is VOIDed. A pick is dropped if its EV (same side, best of DK/Bovada) falls below 3% or its line moves through a key number (NFL spread 3/7/10/14; CFB spread 3/7/10/14/17/21; NFL total 37/41/44/47/51), or its fair-vs-book gap is now over 3 points. No new picks are added. The ledger keeps the original stamp and marks pulled items VOID (0u) with the reason.
  • Props: top 5 per game by model − market, skipping players ESPN lists as Out/Doubtful/IR at log time. Logged once per game when kick is within 24h, as research. Anytime TD is scored separately from yards/receptions, and the record is shown next to expected wins (model vs market). Game-day runs refresh lines older than 12h when the budget allows.
  • Matchups (NFL + FBS, info only): opponent-adjusted unit ranks from nflverse play-by-play (pass/run offense and defense by EPA, pass protection vs pass rush by pressure rate, explosives, red zone), plain-English mismatches, style labels, results vs similar foes and common opponents, and the model's lean vs the market line. It never changes a board pick, because it failed its pre-registered test. No Odds API credits are used.
  • Sim (NFL + FBS, info only): 1,000 simulated games per upcoming game, starting from the de-vigged market spread/total, with shrunk matchup adjustments (means from the matchup leans, red-zone TD/FG mix, pace), played drive by drive and weighted to NFL/CFB key-number frequencies. Shows win/cover/over % vs market and score ranges. Lines are the free nflverse schedule (NFL, incl. next week) and the ESPN scoreboard (FBS). Never changes a pick.
  • Watch line (not pre-registered, never a pick): CFB totals from the lightly-shrunk matchup model (|lean| ≥ 1.5), tracked on paper on the Sim tab, because it was the one sensitivity cell that looked good (166-131 in 2024–25).
  • NBA / NHL (info only): the tabs show unit ranks, plain-English strength-vs-weakness mismatches, goalie/rest notes, similar-style and common-opponent numbers, and a market-anchored sim (NHL: Poisson regulation + empty net + OT/shootout off the de-vigged ML and total; NBA: possession sim off the de-vigged spread and total). Both show win %, puck line/cover %, over %, 80% score ranges and the delta vs the market after the shrunk matchup lean. Both failed their pre-registered tests, so nothing there is a pick or gets logged. Odds: one pull per sport per CT day (4 credits), set aside by the 9am run. NHL starters are projected from recent starts (backup on night 2 of a back-to-back), never assumed confirmed.
  • Grading: ESPN finals and box scores. Each game settles as soon as it's final. A player who played but recorded nothing in that stat is graded as 0; only a player missing from the box score (DNP) is VOID. VOIDs are excluded from W-L-P and units and counted separately.

Credits

The Odds API budget is a hard cap of 40 credits/day: odds cost 6 per sport (us+eu × ML/spread/total). The 9am run reserves 4 credits per kickoff window that has an official item (max 12) for the rechecks; backup recheck crons are no-ops unless an unstarted official item kicks within ~70 minutes. Props get the rest, at 1 per market per game, round-robined across games within 54h of kick. The build used 1,240 credits for historical snapshots.
